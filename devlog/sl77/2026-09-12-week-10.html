Week 10 — A Little More Room to Relax

September 12, 2026

The ship now has upgrades for sofas, dining tables, and bunk beds, giving the captain more ways to prepare for hungry, tired, and uncomfortable passengers.

What changed

  • More places to settle in. You can now unlock an extra sofa, dining table, and bunk bed. That means more room for passengers to eat, rest, and recover during a busy trip.
  • Better furniture, happier needs. Sofa upgrades restore comfort faster, upgraded dining tables improve hunger recovery from meals, and upgraded bunks help passengers recover sleep faster. Each furniture upgrade also increases its maximum condition by half.
  • More choices for your earnings. Furniture improvements cost 12 credits each, while an extra facility costs 20. Both require three likes to unlock access. The kitchen upgrade now requires one like, giving the challenge rewards a more direct role in preparing the ship.
  • Expansion brings upkeep. Maintenance service prices now account for the extra facilities you activate. Another sofa gives passengers somewhere else to relax, but it also adds to the bill when you pay to fix all sofas.
  • An easier shopping screen. The upgrade menu now scrolls properly, with more room for names and descriptions and styled scrollbars. The ship’s outer hull also gained transparency.

Behind the scenes

It was a lovely opportunity to bring a little family collaboration into the game. After last week’s first main menu, the new logo gives the title screen its own identity. So now we have both a logo AND a title screen. Yay! 🚀✨🦥
